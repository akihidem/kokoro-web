The problem

AI doesn’t know “you”

General-purpose AI answers everyone the same way. It doesn’t know your cognitive characteristics, your communication tendencies, or what matters to you.

⚠️

“Please consult a professional” is repeated excessively, and the advice you actually need is thin

💬

The tone and format you asked for stop being followed as the conversation goes on

🧠

Each time you disclose a cognitive difficulty, the AI’s response keeps changing and isn’t consistent

🔒

You tried writing custom instructions, but can’t tell whether they are clinically appropriate

How it works

One file, “translated” by a psychologist

A psychologist converts what is learned from test results and interviews into behavioral instructions. The raw data of clinical assessment findings is never passed to the AI.

1

Assessment

A Licensed Public Psychologist identifies your cognitive characteristics and communication tendencies through interviews and psychological testing.

2

Writing kokoro.md

The findings are translated into “accommodation instructions for the AI,” written like “Don’t give me several tasks at once” or “Use concrete steps.” You review the content and give your consent.

3

Set it up in each AI

Just paste it into Claude Projects, ChatGPT Custom Instructions, or Gemini Gems. No dedicated app is needed.

4

Regular updates

You look back regularly with the psychologist and update kokoro.md as things change.

Design principles

Four promises we keep

Raw findings are never handed over

Raw test scores, diagnostic names, and the source text of clinical assessment findings are not sent to the AI. They stay under the psychologist’s control.

Transparency

kokoro.md is written in words you can read and understand. Anything you don’t agree with can be corrected or deleted.

Use the AI you already have

No new app is needed. You simply set it up in the AI you already use.

Ongoing support from a psychologist

It isn’t made once and then left alone. The psychologist updates it while listening to how your experience changes.
